# Week 1 Day 5 - Employee Data Analysis Report

## 1. Problem Statement
The objective is to analyze employee data and understand salary differences across departments and employee records.

## 2. Dataset Description
The dataset contains 8 employee records with 5 columns:
- Employee ID
- Name
- Department
- Salary
- City

## 3. Three Key Insights
1. The Finance department has the highest average salary at ₹90,000, based on one employee.
2. The IT department has the highest number of employees, with 3 employees.
3. Employee salaries range from ₹30,000 to ₹90,000.

## 4. Two Recommendations
1. Review salary differences across departments to ensure fair compensation.
2. Conduct further employee data analysis to understand salary distribution and workforce structure.

## 5. Limitations
- The dataset contains only 8 employee records.
- The dataset does not include employee experience or joining dates.
- The Finance department has only one employee, so its average salary is not representative of a larger group.

## Conclusion
Five visualizations were created using Matplotlib and Seaborn to understand employee salary distribution and department-wise differences.
